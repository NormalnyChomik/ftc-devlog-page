[chapter]★ The future of FTC![/chapter]

Hello everyone, Isn’t this quite a strange place to be reading the news! With all the new things that just happened everywhere, all at once, I think you may be able to tell that some big things are happening with the game… so what exactly are these big things?

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

Behind the scenes, there has been a rising, bubbling wave of discussions regarding the state of the game, server, and team. Evidently, there were a lot of problems to address, some more difficult than others, which have now made us see the game under an entirely different lens. That’s what I’m going to explain for the first half of this log, our new vision of FTC:

To start things off with the most fundamental change, which we are a little afraid might have people a little split, we are adopting a much more progressive view of the game, rather than a conservative one. That’s to say, moving forwards, we will be a lot more eager to change parts of the game in order to make it better and — most importantly — more fun to play, even if that means altering what could be pretty iconic chunks of FTC.

Understandably, that sounds pretty scary, but I can promise the fact that any changes that may come to the game will have a LOT of respect towards FTC’s legacy. We plan to preserve the game’s charm and special quirks at all costs! You’ll actually hear more about this in the near future!

This letting-go of the past also means that any future content that was set up by the old team may or may not be abandoned. You should take teasers and rumours from the past with a grain of salt – is what I’m trying to say – as the choices and plans that built the foundation of those teasers were put down yeaaarssss ago, some of which may have been in poor judgement… in hindsight…

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

Dex+ has also shifted quite a lot in the tumultuous swirly and twirly couple of days leading up to this announcement.

While working on the game and brainstorming over potential content, we stumbled across a pretty serious problem:


[i][center]The developers had two different visions for the game, and those two visions could hardly coexist.[/center][/i]


One half of the developers wanted to keep development of the game fun, after all, working on FTC is mostly a passion project and the game was born out of that fun mindset. This group is now fully responsible for creating the dex+ chomiks and they won’t be following the modern quality assurance standards, at the benefit of more frequent updates.

The other half wanted to make sure that the last few planned chomiks will be as good as they can be, and that once FTC is finished, it can be remembered in a great light. As such, this team will take a much more critical approach to development, with a large focus on quality. You can expect this team to release updates such as our last areas or chomiks like Awakened Chomik.

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

On that topic, some of the things far out on the horizon that a lot of you are looking forward to are the topic of a bunch of questions. One thing I can say is that, while we are planning to release a few chomiks more difficult than anything the game has seen before, our main priority is quality over difficulty, which also means the gameplay might take a surprising turn! Hopefully those updates will be well received ★

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

Now that you’ve been made aware of all this background bonanza, it may be easier to understand why the dex+ update is so late. The shift in perspective on the game has also, naturally, affected this release, which is why there’s a lot more work to do there than we previously considered… when we said the update would release in a week or two 🥲

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

The last thing I have to address when it comes to the future of the game, is this very website! Pretty cool isn’t it? Well, evidently, we didn’t make an entire website for one announcement. We thought it would be a good idea to – when the time is right – release a devlog explaining our design philosophy when it comes to hot topics that people may not fully trust us with. I hope these will be able to prove that we are being super considerate about everything and  not powering through new content blindly!

[chapter]★ The future of the server![/chapter]

With the chaos of this announcement, those who are keen may have noticed things starting to spontaneously move around them! That is because our conversations about the future weren’t just about the game, but the community as well.

Undeniably, the community has had its ups and downs throughout the years. Whether right now we find ourselves in another down or another up is hard to tell, but we can at least make sure the tracks in front of us are going up⮭. That’s why we are putting down some pillars to make sure the community can stay UP⮭ !

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

First things first, we want to introduce some rules pertaining to recent developments. After some lengthy debating, the staff have collectively agreed to put a ban on all AI generated media. Right alongside that, we also want to put an end to very low quality humour that might be borderline insensitive, take for example jokes about Jeffery Epstein or Israel.

Now, we are fully expecting some people to find this ban excessive but it’s important people understand why we are doing this. The internet manages to paint these topics as things that are purely humorous, maybe even fictional, but they carry an inherent gravity. Keep in mind the essence of these “jokes” is based on real and undeniably horrible, real events. Ignoring the context tends to bring a certain audience into a server that may incidentally be insensitive to the people around them as well. Not only that but encouraging low quality humour will eventually lead to people pushing the limits of what they can or can't say. By taking action against this type of media, we hope that the members don’t just avoid sending shifty media relating to real world events in order to not get in trouble, but because they understand that it will inevitably make the server a worse place. We understand that it definitely isn't the intention of most people to trivialize or mock real world tragedy. We assume that most of you don't get on here with this intent, naturally, but it is still the unfortunate reality of what some people joke about. We do not want you guys to feel bad or guilty for having made these kinds of jokes, as most of us too have been there at some point. What we want is for you guys to grow, realize the fault in this kind of humor, and stray away from it.

We understand that the banning of any form of humor is a tough stance to take upon an entire community, but think about it as a fellowship too. People will inevitably be joining this server and see that people joke about this messed up stuff, grow a horrible impression of us as a whole, leave, and potentially spread word around about what kind of a community we have. We want a more positive community with more understanding, and we feel this is an important decision to reach that goal.

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

You guys aren’t the only peeps having to conform to new rules! To make sure things in the server are kept fair and clean, we also put forward some staff rules as well, so you can expect — for example — tickets to be a lot more fun and helpful.

We also want to shift our focus onto taking stronger action against instances of homophobia, transphobia or ableism, even if they may be rare in the server.

To help with this, we strongly recommend that whenever you or someone around you feels bad because of a rude comment, you should open up a ticket and send us some screenshots, we’ll take a look at it!

[asset/images/LineBreak.png]

We also added some guidelines to posting suggestions! The reason I’m pointing these out specifically despite the fact we pretty much modernized every other ruleset in the server, is because I want to make sure that everyone knows… we do ACTUALLY read the suggestions! Matter of fact we have already added plenty of them to a to-be-released build, and we could add even more if the members understand what we value most in suggestions!
